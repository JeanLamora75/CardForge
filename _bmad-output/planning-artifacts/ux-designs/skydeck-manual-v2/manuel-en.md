# Admiral’s manual — V2

Editorial draft for integration

1. **Your mission** — Take command of your fleet.
2. **The four factions** — Identify forces and strategic units.
3. **Fleet setup** — Discover how the forces are assembled.
4. **The battle cycle** — Master the four phases.
5. **Combat strategies** — Choose the right maneuver.
6. **Free corsairs** — Use the Jokers’ copying ability.
7. **Battle outcome** — Understand the ending conditions.

## 1. Your mission

<!-- id: mission ; chapter: 1 ; visual: manual/skydeck/admiral-mission -->

*The skies are at war. Admiral, your fleet must regain control of the aerial routes.*

Clear every deployed enemy and every enemy in the deck. Capture units to strengthen your fleet. No face card or ace may enter enemy captures, even if it belongs to the enemy.

**Tactical advice :** Plan for the next encounter instead of simply maximizing firepower.

---

## 2. Sky pirates and Navy

<!-- id: factions ; chapter: 2 ; visual: hearts-4,diamonds-4 -->

*Two forces contest the aerial routes.*

Sky pirates carry red sails and a skull. The Navy bears blue colors and an anchor. To capture, engage units of the target faction. Factions have no special powers.

**Tactical advice :** Identify factions by their emblem, not just color.

---

## 3. Guild and Knights

<!-- id: factions-other ; chapter: 2 ; visual: clubs-4,spades-4 -->

*Merchants protect their convoys; knights defend their floating cities.*

The Merchant guild bears green and scales. The Sky knights bear gold and a star. Units numbered 2–10 have the power shown on their shield.

**Tactical advice :** A varied fleet offers more capture options without guaranteeing a legal attack.

---

## 4. Strategic units

<!-- id: strategic ; chapter: 2 ; visual: diamonds-11,diamonds-12,diamonds-13,diamonds-1 -->

*Officers and legendary ships are the pillars of your campaign.*

Lieutenant (Jack): 11; Commander (Queen): 12; Admiral (King): 13; legendary ship (Ace): 14. Capture them to strengthen your reserves. If any enters enemy captures, defeat is immediate.

**Tactical advice :** A recall to your reserves is not a permanent loss.

---

## 5. Gathering the forces

<!-- id: setup ; chapter: 3 ; visual: manual/skydeck/fleet-assembly -->

*Your fleet begins with modest forces.*

Your reinforcement deck contains four units each of power 2, 3 and 4, plus two free corsairs: 14 cards. The other 40 units form the enemy deck. Both decks are shuffled separately.

**Tactical advice :** Your active fleet starts empty; the first units arrive during Reinforcements.

---

## 6. First contact

<!-- id: setup-line ; chapter: 3 ; visual: diagram:initial-line -->

*The lookout reports the first enemy sails.*

Four enemies are revealed from right to left, positions 1–4. Face cards and aces revealed during setup only return to the bottom of their deck. Empty slots are filled during the first deployment.

**Tactical advice :** In normal play, Skydeck handles these operations for you.

---

## 7. The battle cycle

<!-- id: cycle ; chapter: 4 ; visual: diagram:phases -->

*Each round follows a fixed order.*

Deployment: automatic. Reorganization: choose units to recall if a choice exists. Reinforcements: automatic. Combat: choose action A, B or C. In training, phases may be confirmed manually.

**Tactical advice :** The game automates mandatory operations, never your combat decisions.

---

## 8. Deployment

<!-- id: enemy ; chapter: 4 ; visual: diagram:enemy-shift -->

*Enemy forces take position.*

Visible units shift right, preserving their relative order. Empty slots are filled from the enemy deck as far as possible. Strategic units can now remain on the board.

**Tactical advice :** Pay special attention to position 1, at the far right.

---

## 9. Reorganization

<!-- id: discard ; chapter: 4 ; visual: spades-4,spades-3 -->

*Admiral, adapt your formation to the forces before you.*

Select zero to all units, then recall them to your reserves. With no selection, maintain formation. Recalled units are not destroyed and may return. If the fleet is empty, this phase is skipped automatically in normal play.

**Tactical advice :** Compare your factions with visible enemies before recalling a unit.

---

## 10. Reinforcements

<!-- id: draw ; chapter: 4 ; visual: hearts-2,clubs-3,diamonds-4,joker-1 -->

*New sails join your fleet.*

Your fleet is refilled to four units. If your personal deck runs out during this operation, your reserves are shuffled to rebuild it. Captured and recalled units can return. In normal play, dealing is automatic.

**Tactical advice :** Reserves and enemy captures are two different destinations.

---

## 11. Command

<!-- id: combat ; chapter: 4 ; visual: diagram:combat-choices -->

*The moment of decision has arrived.*

Choose exactly one maneuver: A Capture, B Sacrifice or C Push back. Prepare your selections, then confirm the legal action. The next round begins after resolution unless the battle is over.

**Tactical advice :** Each maneuver has different consequences: check where the units will go.

---

## 12. A · Capture

<!-- id: a ; chapter: 5 ; visual: diagram:a:diamonds-3,diamonds-5|diamonds-8 -->

*Turn enemy strength to your advantage.*

Choose a visible enemy. Engage one or more units of its faction whose combined power meets or exceeds its own. Example: Navy 3 + Navy 5 against Navy 8. All three units join your reserves.

**Tactical advice :** Captures strengthen your deck over time.

---

## 13. Insufficient power

<!-- id: a-power ; chapter: 5 ; visual: diagram:a-invalid:diamonds-3,diamonds-5|diamonds-9 -->

*A poorly prepared attack cannot be launched.*

Navy 3 + Navy 5 total 8. Against Navy 9, one point is missing. The attack is rejected; no card is spent.

**Tactical advice :** Change target or engage another compatible unit if possible.

---

## 14. Incompatible factions

<!-- id: a-faction ; chapter: 5 ; visual: diagram:a-invalid:diamonds-3,diamonds-5|hearts-8 -->

*Power alone cannot coordinate an attack.*

Navy 3 + Navy 5 cannot capture Pirate 8 despite a total power of 8. Engaged ordinary units must belong to the target faction.

**Tactical advice :** Check emblems before comparing power.

---

## 15. B · Sacrifice

<!-- id: b ; chapter: 5 ; visual: diagram:b:diamonds-2|hearts-9 -->

*Accept a loss to remove an immediate threat.*

Choose one personal unit. It and the enemy in position 1, at the right, permanently enter enemy captures. Example: Navy 2 and Pirate 9. If either is strategic, you immediately lose.

**Tactical advice :** Check both power values before sacrificing.

---

## 16. C · Push back

<!-- id: c ; chapter: 5 ; visual: diagram:c:diamonds-2,hearts-3|spades-13 -->

*Drive back a threat without capturing it.*

Choose two distinct personal units: they permanently enter enemy captures. Choose a visible enemy: it returns to the bottom of its deck. Example: Navy 2 and Pirate 3 to push back Knight 13. Sacrificing a strategic unit causes defeat.

**Tactical advice :** The pushed-back threat may return; you have not conquered it.

---

## 17. Free corsairs

<!-- id: joker ; chapter: 6 ; visual: diagram:joker:diamonds-4,joker-1|diamonds-8 -->

*Corsairs adapt to the forces they accompany.*

During capture A, a Joker copies the faction and power of an ordinary unit actually engaged. Navy 4 + Joker provide 8 Navy power. A Joker alone provides no independent power.

**Tactical advice :** Choose an engaged reference compatible with the target.

---

## 18. Corsair alliance

<!-- id: joker-double ; chapter: 6 ; visual: diagram:joker:diamonds-4,joker-1,joker-2|diamonds-12 -->

*Two corsairs can combine their talents.*

Both Jokers may copy the same engaged unit. Navy 4 + two Jokers provide 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 Navy power. They cannot attack alone. Jokers used in a successful capture join reserves with the other units.

**Tactical advice :** Two Jokers without a reference unit are not a legal attack.

---

## 19. Glory or downfall

<!-- id: ending ; chapter: 7 ; visual: boards/skydeck/victory-portrait,boards/skydeck/defeat-portrait -->

*The final battle decides the fate of your campaign.*

Victory: both the enemy line and deck are empty after a combat action resolves. Immediate defeat: any face card or ace enters enemy captures, whether personal or enemy. Remaining personal units do not prevent victory.

**Tactical advice :** Protect strategic units even when they are facing you.

---

## 20. Exceptional situations

<!-- id: limits ; chapter: 7 ; visual: diagram:hidden-zones -->

*Some situations require particular vigilance.*

If reinforcements and reserves cannot refill your fleet, the current version pauses the battle without recording defeat. Sacrifice B is unavailable if position 1 is empty. Decks, reserves and enemy captures remain hidden: only counts and visible units are public.

**Tactical advice :** Rely on visible information. No official score is added.
